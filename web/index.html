Click the screen and type this program. Press Enter after each line, then type RUN.

10 FOR I=1 TO 5

LIST shows your program. Retype a line number to replace it, or enter the number alone to delete it. Use arrow keys, Home, End, Backspace and Delete to edit. Escape stops execution.

Weave BASIC is our own small interpreter, with a Rust firmware service. It is not Microsoft MSX BASIC. Type HELP for commands. Enter returns from graphics.

Bring a cartridge

Download our original test cartridge, then select it above. Hold Space to play its PSG tone. AB cartridges boot in slot 1. Auto recognizes plain images; for a MegaROM choose its documented mapper before selecting the file. No fixed upload size cap; available memory and mapper address range apply. SRAM variants and SCC sound are unsupported. Files stay on your device and are never uploaded or saved.

Use your own system ROM

Load a lawfully obtained 32 KiB MSX1 BIOS/BASIC ROM. It stays in this browser and is never uploaded or saved. Original BIOS APIs are needed by many commercial cartridges.

Arrow keys + Z/X also act as joystick 1. Audio starts only after you enable it. Reset restarts the selected ROMs; Built-in BASIC unloads both.
